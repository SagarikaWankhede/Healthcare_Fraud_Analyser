Syllabus Coverage & Multi-Technique Architecture

Four foundational data science techniques combined into one coherent decision pipeline.

Relational Consolidation & Provider-Level Partitioning

Joining 4 CMS relational files and rolling up 558,211 receipts into 48 provider behavior indicators.

Strict Provider Split
Relational Join

Consolidated Inpatient claims, Outpatient claims, and Beneficiary KYC records using BeneID and Provider foreign keys. Imputed missing reimbursement deductibles to \$0.

Leakage Prevention

Split 80/20 strictly by Provider ID (4,328 train / 1,082 test). Never split by individual claim, preventing the model from memorizing doctor IDs or clinic identifiers.

Physician Overlap

Engineered doctor network features: ratios where the Attending Physician is also the Operating Physician, physician claim overload, and chronic disease prevalence ratios across 11 CMS conditions.

Technique A: Claim Reimbursement Cost Modeling & Residuals

Predicting expected claim amounts and quantifying excessive billing deviations.

R² = 0.5763

We trained a HistGradientBoostingRegressor on 424,435 training claims to predict the standard reimbursement amount based on patient age, chronic illnesses, inpatient stay duration, and procedure intensity:

Residual_i = Actual_Reimbursed_i - Predicted_Expected_i

If a provider consistently logs large positive residuals, they are billing significantly more than patient clinical profiles justify. We aggregate these into total excess reimbursement and positive residual ratio per provider.

Overbilling Residual Signal

Provides the classifier with a clinical baseline cost expectation. Enabled the model to boost PR-AUC by +1.61% over volume-only baseline.

Technique B: Unsupervised Clustering & Anomaly Detection

Hierarchical typology discovering clinic operational scale and DBSCAN density outlier detection.

No Labels Used

Hierarchical Clustering (Ward linkage): Discovered 4 distinct operational archetypes (e.g. small outpatient practices, high-volume diagnostic centers, large inpatient hospitals), giving the model normalized expectations per tier.

DBSCAN Density Anomaly Detection: Evaluated multi-dimensional behavioral density. Isolated 91 anomalous providers (2.1%) sitting outside standard density clusters (flagged as dbscan_outlier_flag = 1).

Inductive Core Assignment:

Test providers are evaluated against fitted training core samples using nearest neighbors, guaranteeing zero test set contamination.

Technique C: In-Fold Apriori Association Rule Mining

Treating diagnostic and procedure codes as transaction baskets to flag fraudulent treatment combinations.

In-Fold Only

We treated claim diagnosis codes (D_*) and procedure codes (P_*) as transaction baskets. To prevent data leakage, rules were mined strictly within the training fold (153K fraud claims vs 271K legitimate claims).

We isolated 12 statistically significant combinations with an Enrichment Ratio (Lift) ≥ 1.8x in fraudulent claims compared to legitimate claims.

Top Discovered Combination:

D_2851 + D_4019 (Anemia + Hypertension)

Enrichment: 2.52x higher occurrence in fraudulent providers

Derived Provider Features:

  • suspicious_code_claim_count: Total claims containing flagged combinations.
  • suspicious_code_claim_ratio: Proportion of claims with flagged combinations.
  • suspicious_combo_variety: Number of distinct suspicious pairs utilized.
